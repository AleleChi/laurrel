### 4.3 Cleaning Deep-Dive
- **Scope Included:**
  - Detailed dusting of reachable light fixtures, blinds, and window sills
  - Deep scrubbing of tubs, tiles, showers, sinks, and toilets
  - Kitchen appliance exteriors, countertops, backsplashes, and cabinet faces
  - Vacuuming carpets and washing hard floor surfaces with fresh pads
  - Interior oven, refrigerator, and cabinet cleans available upon request
- **Customer Preparation Tips:**
  - Ensure running water and electrical power are active on clean day
  - Clear personal items off bathroom counters and kitchen countertops
  - Defrost the freezer the night before if interior fridge cleaning is requested

### 4.4 Moving Deep-Dive
- **Scope Included:**
  - Careful disassembly of bed frames and standard tables if needed
  - Padded wrapping of wood, glass, and upholstered furniture pieces
  - Floor runners installed at both origin and destination doorways
  - Loading, transit in clean enclosed moving trucks, and placement in designated rooms
- **Customer Preparation Tips:**
  - Tape and label all carton boxes with their destination room
  - Reserve building elevators and loading docks well in advance
  - Keep important personal documents, jewellery, and medications with you

### 4.5 Storage Deep-Dive
- **Scope Included:**
  - Direct pickup from your home or apartment by our moving crew
  - Furniture stays wrapped in protective pads throughout storage
  - Clean, dry, indoor holding environment
  - Scheduled delivery directly into your new residence
- **Customer Preparation Tips:**
  - Ensure all items packed in boxes are securely taped
  - Do not pack perishable foods, hazardous liquids, or batteries in stored boxes
  - Label cartons containing items you might need early access to
